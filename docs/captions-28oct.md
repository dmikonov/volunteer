## EN (@surftribe_world)
Sound familiar? A wave hits and your own board flies back at you. Usually it's how you're holding it.

Walking out through the foam:
Board at your side, nose pointing at the wave
Never sideways in front of you
When the foam comes, grab both rails and push the nose over

Stuck on something? The coach in the SurfTribe app answers.
