Paddling like crazy and going nowhere? Check how you're lying. Dead flat on the board, your arms mostly slap the water.

Try this:
Lift your chest a little, like a seal looking up
Eyes on the horizon, not on the board
Long, deep strokes all the way to your hip

Not sure your chest is up? Get a friend to film you and ask the AI coach in SurfTribe.
