## EN (@surftribe_world)
Small bumps throw you off because your knees are locked. A straight leg can't give, so every bump goes right into your body.

1. Keep your knees soft the whole ride, not only on the pop-up
2. Sit low, like there's a chair behind you
3. Film a wave and check the knee angle in SurfTribe. Around 175° means your legs are almost straight
