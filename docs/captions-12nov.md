## EN 15.11, rchan_en («Nobody tells you why good surfers paddle out where no waves break», Т301)
Good surfers paddle out where no waves break. That calm strip is a rip, and they use it as a free ride.

Find the gap from the sand, where the waves don't break
Paddle out in it, then slide sideways to the peak before you pass it

New to surfing? Stay out of rips. If one grabs you, don't fight it, paddle parallel to the beach.

Bigger swell means a stronger rip. SurfTribe shows the wave height for your beach.
