Stop paddling into the calm gap. It can be a rip current.

1. Watch the water for 10 minutes first
2. Caught in one? Stay on your board. Don't fight it
3. Paddle parallel to the beach and raise a hand

The signs: a gap with no breaking waves, water moving out to sea.
