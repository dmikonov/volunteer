## EN (@surftribe_world)
Here's what happens when you hold your board sideways in the whitewater: the wave throws it right back at you.

How to hold it:
1. Board at your side, nose pointing at the wave
2. Never across in front of you
3. When the foam comes, grab both rails and push the nose over it
4. Foam gone: lie down and paddle out

Save this for your first day in the foam.
